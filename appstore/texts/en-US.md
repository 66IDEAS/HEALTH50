## Description

```
Health50 exports the health data on your iPhone as plain JSON files, so you can use it with the tools you choose: a spreadsheet, a script or an AI assistant.

SAVE A COPY
Tap "Save to Files…" and choose the last 30 days, the last 12 months or your full history. Health50 writes one JSON file to the location you pick.

KEEP IT CURRENT
Turn on automatic export and Health50 keeps a folder in your own Google Drive up to date: one file per month, updated in the background when something changed.

YOU DECIDE WHAT IS EXPORTED
iOS asks which health data Health50 may read. Share all of it or only a part. Health50 exports only what you allow, and it only reads. It never changes your health records.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No Health50 account and no Health50 servers.
• No analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
• Data goes straight from your iPhone to the place you pick.
• In Google Drive, Health50 can only work with the files and folders it created itself.

A FORMAT YOU CAN READ
The files are plain JSON with clear names, Apple's own identifiers for the data types and a unit on every value. They cover activity, heart, sleep, workouts with routes, body measurements, nutrition, mindfulness, ECGs and more: over 190 data types. Clinical records are not included.

FREE AND OPEN SOURCE
Health50 is free, with no purchases. Its source code, the description of the file format and its privacy policy are public:
github.com/66IDEAS/HEALTH50

Health50 is not a medical device and gives no medical advice. It reads from the Health app on your iPhone. Google Drive is a trademark of Google LLC.
```
